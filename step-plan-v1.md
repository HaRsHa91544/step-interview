# STEP Interview Preparation Plan - Version 1

## 1. Learn & Solve — 2 Hours (Minimum)

- 1 hour: Programming
- 1 hour: Aptitude
- Solve problems independently or in groups.

## 2. Mock Interviews — 1.5 Hours (Minimum)

- 9 members divided into 3 groups
- Each group interviews minimum of 30 mins for every member 
- For every member:
  - Ask 1 unexpected or different question (to make them confused or fearful).
  - OR ask them to explain 1 different, unfamiliar concept and then apply it.

### Interview Process:

- Follow these steps for every programming problem:

1. Understand the problem: Make sure you understand what the problem is asking.
2. Clarify: Explain the problem in your own words and confirm your understanding with the interviewer.
3. Basic approach: Explain your initial approach before writing code.
4. Write the code: Implement your basic solution.
5. Dry run: Test your code with examples and understand where it breaks.
   - Only the interviewee should identify where the code breaks. Don't reveal the mistake.
6. Correct or optimize: Fix the identified mistake or optimize the basic solution.

### Three Important Rules

1. Don't memorize solutions.
2. Don't avoid unfamiliar problems.
3. Don't hide mistakes.

## 3. Everyday: GitHub Log

Every day, answer these three questions:

1. What mistake did I make today?
2. What did I learn today?
3. What will I do differently tomorrow?
